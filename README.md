## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

*Let farmers and FPOs create profiles and list produce with quantity, grade, location, and harvest date.
*Connect them directly with households, retailers, restaurants, and bulk buyers.
*Show local market prices so farmers can make informed decisions and buyers can compare offers.
*Allow buyers to place advance orders, helping farmers plan harvest and sales.
*Combine orders from nearby buyers to make transport more affordable.
*Provide order updates, digital payment records, and a way to report quality or delivery issues.

## Technical Approach

*Build a mobile-friendly platform with simple screens and local-language support.
*Include farmer registration, produce listings, buyer search, ordering, and order history.
*Add verified profiles and ratings to help farmers and buyers build trust.
*Use current market prices and past orders to suggest prices and forecast demand.
*Group orders by crop and location to plan shared deliveries.
*Provide delivery status updates and digital payment confirmation.
*Offer assisted onboarding through FPOs or local service centres for users who need help.

## Feasibility and Viability

*Start with a small pilot in one region and a few commonly grown crops.
*Work with FPOs, local buyers, collection centres, and transport providers.
*Use existing phones and transport networks to keep startup costs manageable.
*Train a small group of farmers and buyers, then gather feedback before expanding.
*Address inconsistent internet access with a lightweight app and assisted order entry.
*Reduce payment and quality disputes with clear terms, transaction records, and complaint support.
*Track operating costs and revenue from service fees or delivery charges to assess long-term viability.


## Impact and Benefits

*Farmers can reach more buyers and may retain a larger share of the selling price.
*Buyers can compare offers and source produce closer to where it is grown.
*Advance orders can help farmers plan harvesting and reduce unsold produce.
*Shared deliveries can lower per-order transport costs and improve route efficiency.
*Clear listings and order records can improve transparency and trust.
*Better demand information can help FPOs plan collection and distribution.
*Reduced delays can help deliver fresher produce and limit spoilage.
